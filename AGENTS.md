# Writing preferences

Use direct, concrete phrasing in English and Greek across articles, captions,
diagrams, UI copy and chatbot replies. State the subject and explain what it does.

Avoid rhetorical contrasts such as “X, not Y”, “not X but Y”, “this isn't about
X, it's about Y” and Greek equivalents such as «Χ, όχι Υ» or «δεν είναι Χ, είναι
Υ». Avoid introducing an unwanted alternative through “rather than” or “instead
of”. Rewrite the point as a direct statement. Keep necessary factual comparisons,
limitations and ordinary negation accurate; describe changes or differences
explicitly when they matter.

Preserve publication abstracts from the actual papers, including their existing
translations. Exempt them, direct quotations and attributed source text from
editorial style rewrites. Apply writing preferences to original site copy.

Avoid em dashes in prose. Use natural Greek, retaining established English
technical terms where they read naturally. Preserve the author's factual meaning
and the distinct delivery of each chatbot persona.

When editing reviewed chatbot answers, retain their original generation
attribution and record editorial revisions separately. Recheck affected evidence
and prompt fingerprints before approving the cache again.
