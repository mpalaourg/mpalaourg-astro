# Technology marks used in the project illustration

Local copies of public brand assets, retrieved 24 October 2026. The illustration
embeds these SVG paths directly; viewing it makes no third-party requests.
Monochrome colors and display sizes are adapted to the diagram background.

- Astro, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Cloudflare and OpenRouter:
  [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), SVGs from
  `icons/{astro,typescript,tailwindcss,cloudflare,openrouter}.svg`.
  [Simple Icons license](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md).
- LangSmith: [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons), static SVG
  `@lobehub/icons-static-svg/icons/langsmith.svg`.
  [Lobe Icons license](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/LICENSE).
- Pinecone: the logo mark in the official [Pinecone newsroom](https://www.pinecone.io/newsroom/)
  media-kit section, retained with its original viewBox and paths.

Brand marks identify technologies used by the project and do not imply endorsement.
